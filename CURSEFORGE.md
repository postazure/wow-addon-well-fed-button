# Well Fed Button – CurseForge listing

## Summary (short field)

Never forget your food buff again. One button holds all your Well Fed food and glows when you're missing the buff. Choose which food it eats first (smallest stack, last eaten or cheapest), drag it anywhere, and bind a hotkey to eat in one press.

## Description

**Well Fed Button** puts all the Well Fed food in your bags into one small button. Click it to eat. When you aren't Well Fed, the button glows so you remember to eat before the next pull.

### Features

- **One button for all your buff food.** It finds every food in your bags that gives Well Fed and shows how many you have.
- **Expand to see them all.** Right-click to open a bar with each Well Fed food, then click any of them to eat it. Click anywhere else to close the bar.
- **Glows when you need to eat.** An animated border shows while you're alive and don't have the Well Fed buff. You can pick the glow color (blue, gold, green or white) or turn it off.
- **You choose which food the button shows.** Pick the smallest stack (to clear bag space), the food you ate most recently, or the cheapest food first.
- **Greys out when you can't eat,** like an action bar button, for example in combat.
- **Hotkeys:** one key to eat and one key to open the bar. Set them in Key Bindings → AddOns.
- **Matches the game's look,** with the same button frame and panel style as the built-in UI.

### Controls

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left-click | Eat |
| Right-click | Open or close the food bar |
| Shift + drag | Move the button |
| Gear button (at the end of the bar) | Settings |

### Slash commands

`/wfb` or `/wellfed`

- `/wfb mode smallest | recent | value` – which food the button shows
- `/wfb dir up | down | left | right` – which way the bar opens
- `/wfb scale <0.5–3>` – button size
- `/wfb glow` – turn the glow on or off
- `/wfb color blue | gold | green | white` – glow color
- `/wfb lock` – lock or unlock the button's position
- `/wfb rescan` – check your bags again
- `/wfb reset` – put the button back in its starting spot

### Notes

- Made for WoW Forever (Classic-era client).
- If you have Bagnon installed, the tooltips use its Shift-click icon.
