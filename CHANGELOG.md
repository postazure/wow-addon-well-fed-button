## 1.0.3

First release.

- One button for all the Well Fed food in your bags, with a count. Left-click to eat.
- Right-click to open a bar with each Well Fed food. Click anywhere else to close it.
- The button glows while you're alive and not Well Fed. Pick the color or turn it off.
- Choose which food the button shows: smallest stack, last eaten, or cheapest.
- Icons grey out when you can't eat, for example in combat.
- Hotkeys to eat and to open the bar, under Key Bindings → AddOns.
- Shift-drag to move the button. The gear at the end of the bar opens settings.
